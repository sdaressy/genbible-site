Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 9, 2026

GenBible ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the GenBible mobile application.

The short version: We collect minimal data. Your reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights stay on your device. We don't sell your data to anyone.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored locally on your device:

  • Reading progress and streaks
  • Bookmarks and highlights
  • App preferences and settings
  • Onboarding selections

This data is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's local storage frameworks. We do not have access to it, and it is not transmitted to any server.

Analytics data:

  • Anonymous app usage events (e.g., screens viewed, features used)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version

We use Amplitude for anonymous analytics to understand how the app is used and improve the experience. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Subscription data:

  • Subscription status and entitlements are managed by Apple and RevenueCat
  • We do not collect or store your payment information — all transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal contact information
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers

3. How We Use Information

The anonymous analytics data we collect is used solely to:

  • Understand which features are most useful
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Improve the app experience

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than improving GenBible.

4. Third-Party Services

GenBible uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store — for processing subscriptions and payments
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • Amplitude — for anonymous analytics (Amplitude Privacy Policy)

These services may collect limited technical data as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

All personal reading data is stored on your device only. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 12 months.

6. Children's Privacy

GenBible does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or personal information to use.

7. Data Security

Since we store data locally on your device and do not maintain user accounts or servers with personal data, the risk of data breaches is minimal. Your device's built-in security protections (passcode, Face ID, etc.) safeguard your app data.

8. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is generally nothing to request deletion of. You can:

  • Delete the app at any time to remove all local data
  • Manage your subscription through Apple's App Store settings
  • Contact us to request deletion of any analytics data associated with your device

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
